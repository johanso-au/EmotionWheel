## Part 1 – Core wheel (verified)

The Feelings Wheel idea comes from Gloria Willcox (1982). This is the widely used three-ring layout by Geoffrey Roberts (2015).
Checked word-for-word against Calm's published copy of the wheel.

- Words that appear twice in the original: Embarrassed, Disappointed, Inferior, Overwhelmed.
- Some reprints (including Calm's) show "Let down → Betrayed, Disrespected", which repeats Disrespected. Roberts' original has "Resentful", kept here.
